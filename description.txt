Up to 4 AI factions (1-6 bots each) play Terraria by vanilla rules while you spectate or play along.
- Gather, craft and build houses, storage and guest rooms; upgrade gear tier by tier to Chlorophyte, equip accessories, drink potions.
- Melee, ranged, magic and summoner bots (minions follow their owner and attack its target).
- Fight every boss from King Slime to Moon Lord: readiness checks, summon rituals (orbs, hellbridge, altar, truffle worm...), credit per faction.
- Chase end-game gear: Ankh Shield, Terraspark Boots, Cell Phone and style picks (Zenith, Vortex Beater, Last Prism...), farming rare drops and souls where they live; climb sky ladders to hunt wyverns for wings.
- Guns and bows use ammo: bots keep a supply (craft, withdraw or buy from the Arms Dealer/Merchant) and switch weapons when out; they earn coins by selling junk and outgrown loot, spend spare money on healing potions and better gear, and drop half their money on death; a quartermaster keeps wood, torches and ammo stocked at base, and teammates hand ammo to whoever runs dry.
- Rescue town NPCs, house them and buy stations and pylons from their shops; anglers fish and turn in Angler quests; monster hunters hang banners, collectors dress mannequins, naturalists cage critters.
- Smarter travel: fly with wings, double-jump, sprint with boots, hook up cliffs; break long falls with a double jump or hook and steer around spots where anyone fell; clear trees, logs and empty chests that block digging or pillaring, dig out of deep caves without a recall item, route through the Lihzahrd temple maze, hop between pylons.
- Modes: Race, Cooperate, War (factions fight but never wreck or rob each other's bases). Faction count, bots per faction and relation are saved per world; the config only sets defaults for new worlds, and /autoterra changes the current one.
Single-player only.

最多 4 个 AI 阵营（每阵营 1-6 人）按原版规则游玩泰拉瑞亚，你可以观战或一起玩。
- 采集、合成、建造房屋、仓库和客房；逐级升级装备直到叶绿，佩戴饰品，喝药水。
- 近战、射手、法师、召唤师四种职业（召唤物跟随主人并攻击其目标）。
- 挑战从史莱姆王到月亮领主的所有 Boss：评估战力，完成召唤仪式（暗影珠、地狱桥、祭坛、松露虫……），按阵营结算击杀。
- 追求终局装备：十字章护盾、泰拉闪耀靴、手机及各职业武器饰品（天顶剑、星旋机枪、最后的棱镜……），去对应环境刷稀有掉落和魂；搭天梯打飞龙做翅膀。
- 枪和弓消耗弹药：角色会囤积弹药（合成、从仓库取或向军火商/商人购买），没弹药时换武器；把杂物和淘汰装备卖给 NPC 赚钱，有余钱时买治疗药水和更好的装备，死亡时掉落一半钱币；由一名“后勤”角色在基地囤积木材、火把和弹药，队友弹药耗尽时会有人把弹药送过去。
- 解救城镇 NPC，为他们盖房，并从商店购买制作站和传送晶塔；爱钓鱼的角色会钓鱼并提交渔夫任务，怪物猎人挂旗帜，收藏家给人体模型穿套装，博物学家抓小动物放进笼子。
- 更聪明的移动：会用翅膀飞行、二段跳、疾跑鞋加速、用钩爪攀上悬崖；长距离坠落时用二段跳或钩爪缓冲，并绕开有人摔伤过的地方；会砍掉挡路的树、倒木并拿走空宝箱后再挖或搭柱子，没有回城道具也能从深洞脱困，穿过丛林神庙迷宫，在传送晶塔之间传送。
- 模式：竞速、合作、敌对（阵营间可互相攻击，但不会破坏或偷取对方基地）。阵营数量、人数和关系跟随世界保存；配置只是新世界的默认值，用 /autoterra 修改当前世界。
仅支持单人模式。
