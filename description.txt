Up to 4 AI factions (1-6 bots each) play Terraria by vanilla rules while you spectate or play along.
- Gather, craft and build houses, storage and guest rooms; upgrade gear tier by tier to Chlorophyte, equip accessories, drink potions.
- Fight every boss from King Slime to Moon Lord: readiness checks, summon rituals (orbs, hellbridge, altar, truffle worm...), credit per faction.
- Chase end-game gear: Ankh Shield, Terraspark Boots, Cell Phone and style picks (Zenith, Vortex Beater, Last Prism...), farming rare drops and souls where they live; climb sky ladders to hunt wyverns for wings.
- Rescue town NPCs, house them and buy stations from their shops; anglers fish and turn in Angler quests.
- Modes: Race, Cooperate, War (factions fight but never wreck or rob each other's bases).
Single-player only.

最多 4 个 AI 阵营（每阵营 1-6 人）按原版规则游玩泰拉瑞亚，你可以观战或一起玩。
- 采集、合成、建造房屋、仓库和客房；逐级升级装备直到叶绿，佩戴饰品，喝药水。
- 挑战从史莱姆王到月亮领主的所有 Boss：评估战力，完成召唤仪式（暗影珠、地狱桥、祭坛、松露虫……），按阵营结算击杀。
- 追求终局装备：十字章护盾、泰拉闪耀靴、手机及各职业武器饰品（天顶剑、星旋机枪、最后的棱镜……），去对应环境刷稀有掉落和魂；搭天梯打飞龙做翅膀。
- 解救城镇 NPC，为他们盖房，并从商店购买制作站；爱钓鱼的角色会钓鱼并提交渔夫任务。
- 模式：竞速、合作、敌对（阵营间可互相攻击，但不会破坏或偷取对方基地）。
仅支持单人模式。
