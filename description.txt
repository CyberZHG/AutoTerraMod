To enable this MOD, select a map and go to Settings -> Mod Configurations -> Auto Terra Factions, then set Enable Bots to On.

Up to 6 AI factions play Terraria by vanilla rules while you spectate or play along.

Note: All testing during development was conducted with 2 factions, each consisting of 6 characters. Selecting too many characters may cause lag or make progression permanently impossible due to resource depletion in the world.

- AI-controlled NPCs can gather resources, craft items, build bases, and upgrade equipment. They also have different weapon preferences.
- The goal is to defeat all bosses up to and including the Moon Lord.
- Settings and NPC data are saved with the world.
- Single-player only.
