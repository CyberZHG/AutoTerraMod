Up to 4 AI factions (1-6 bots each) play Terraria by vanilla rules while you spectate or play along.
- Gather, craft, build bases and upgrade gear; melee, ranged, magic and summoner bots.
- Builders lay flat highways, a rope shaft to the Underworld and small houses at distant boss arenas.
- Fight every boss from King Slime to Moon Lord and chase end-game gear.
- Use ammo, earn and spend money, share supplies with teammates.
- Rescue and house town NPCs; hobbies like fishing, banners, mannequins, critter cages and faction-colored dyes.
- Modes: Race, Cooperate, War. Settings are saved per world (/autoterra).
Single-player only.

最多 4 个 AI 阵营（每阵营 1-6 人）按原版规则游玩泰拉瑞亚，你可以观战或一起玩。
- 采集、合成、建造基地、升级装备；近战、射手、法师、召唤师四种职业。
- 建房者修建水平通道、直通地狱的绳索竖井，并在远处 Boss 场地建小屋。
- 挑战从史莱姆王到月亮领主的所有 Boss，追求终局装备。
- 使用弹药、赚钱花钱、与队友共享物资。
- 解救并安置城镇 NPC；钓鱼、挂旗帜、摆人体模型、养小动物、穿阵营色染料等爱好。
- 模式：竞速、合作、敌对；设置随世界保存（/autoterra）。
仅支持单人模式。
